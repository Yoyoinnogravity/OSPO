Post-Plot QC - Attributes vs SP
Load acquired sequence files (P1/90, P1/11, raw P2/94 or raw P2/11) to extract per-shotpoint attributes and graph them against SP. P2/94 adds streamer depths, pitch/roll/heave, gyro/network observations and GPS heights; P2/11 adds gun depths/pressures/timing, MRU, gyros and GPS heights (auto-grouped by node). Files are processed locally - nothing is uploaded.
QC limits (optional, per survey specs): Out-of-spec shots flagged red on graphs

3D ray tracing (NORSAR-class) — GPU job quote
Water-column Snell tracing stays in the browser. 3D illumination through a user velocity model runs on hired GPU, not this planner host. Pick the algorithm, enter the survey metrics, and we estimate GPU time and a one-off quote — typically far below a yearly 3D modelling seat.
Algorithm
You must provide — 3D migration aperture
This is the half-width of the 3D migration operator at target depth (metres), from your processing spec — not the sail-line length. The velocity model and GPU volume must extend this far beyond the image area on all sides, or edge bins lose dip and illumination is wrong. If the spec gives a max dip instead, enter the angle and we convert: aperture ≈ Z × tan(θ).
Enter the aperture from the processing spec, or a max dip to suggest one. Quote is held until aperture is set.
Products included: first-arrival travel times, illumination / hit count at target, incidence at image points, QC plots. You supply the velocity model plus 3D migration aperture; we do not store the model on the planner VPS.
