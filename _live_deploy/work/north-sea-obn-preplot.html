Case study

North Sea OBN preplot and route plan

2026Candooka OSPO

Summary

A demonstration North Sea ocean-bottom node patch: generate receiver and source preplots, keep turns outside infrastructure and MPAs, then export a plan the vessel and GIS team can open.

Responsibilities

  • Define patch geometry, line spacing and shot interval for OBN source lines
  • Load marine layers (infrastructure, cables, MPAs) against the same map as the preplot
  • Run obstruction-aware route search with a minimum turn radius
  • Estimate duration and cost from the resulting sail order
  • Export KML / Shapefile / GeoJSON for client and contractor GIS

Outcomes

  • Single map holds preplot, route and constraint layers — no split between “design” and “nav”
  • Turn geometry stays outside exclusion zones instead of being cleaned up after export
  • Planner holds the same North Sea-shaped geometry the vessel and GIS team export
  • Exports match the on-screen plan (KML, Shapefile, GeoJSON)
