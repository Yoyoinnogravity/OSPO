Personal profile

Aled Morgan

Designer and founder of Candooka OSPO, a web-based offshore survey planner for marine seismic and geophysical operations.

Candooka is built for client representatives, navigators and survey planners who need preplots, route optimisation, look-ahead timing, tide windows, cost estimates and GIS export without a heavy desktop stack.

Focus

  • Interactive marine GIS and map-first survey design
  • Preplot generation and OBN / 2D / 3D line geometry
  • Route optimisation around obstructions and environmental limits
  • Clear reporting for offshore operations
